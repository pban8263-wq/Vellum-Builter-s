Our Build Process

A clear path from vision to reality

  1. 01

    Site Visit & Brief

    We walk the plot, measure it, and listen to how you actually want to live in it.

  2. 02

    Concept Blueprint

    A first line drawing of the plan — porch, rooms, garden — for you to react to.

  3. 03

    Material Palette

    Stone, timber, brass, plaster — chosen together, before anything is ordered.

  4. 04

    Construction Drawings

    The blueprint becomes buildable — dimensioned, detailed, ready for site.

  5. 05

    Site Execution

    Weekly site visits while it's built, so the drawing and the wall stay honest to each other.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Keys, a walkthrough, and a maintenance note for the year ahead.
